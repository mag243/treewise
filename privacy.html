Treewise

Privacy

Treewise collects nothing about you or your files. It has no account, no analytics and no tracking. This page says what does leave your Mac, and when.

The app

Treewise works on the files on your Mac and on the disks and cloud folders you connect. It sends nothing about them anywhere. What you choose in Settings stays on your Mac.

Checking for updates

Once a day Treewise reads the list of versions, appcast.xml, from this site. Like any request on the internet, that request carries your IP address and the name and version of the app; it sends nothing else about you or your Mac. When there is a new version, Treewise asks before it downloads it from GitHub. You can turn the check off in Settings.

Name suggestions

Suggest Names gets its proposals from add-ons. They run in a separate service on your Mac that has no access to the internet, and see only the files they handle: their names, and only what they need from them. Nothing leaves your Mac.

This site

This site has no cookies, no analytics and no tracking. It is served by GitHub Pages, and the downloads come from GitHub, which sees your IP address as any web host does; see GitHub’s privacy statement. Installing with Homebrew goes through Homebrew, which has its own analytics that you can turn off.

Feedback

An issue you open on GitHub is public, with your GitHub name. Leave out anything you do not want to share.
